What's in a Prompt?

Prompts are how you give instruction to AI models. The more detail you put into a prompt, the better the output is usually. However, as longer prompts also use more tokens, there is a delicate balance of putting the right amount of information in a prompt while making it as short as possible.
Things to avoid in a prompt include:

  • Talking to the AI like it's a person. It's not. It's simply just code that (for now) will listen to your request however you say it
  • Constant use of words like "and". Instead of connecting your commands like that, list them in bullet point format (like this list!)
  • Use simple language. An AI takes more effort to understand prompts full of lingo or jargon.
